# Offline CJK fallback

The unmodified Noto Sans CJK SC Regular font follows IBM Plex and Noto Sans Symbols 2 in the native font families. It supplies common Chinese, Japanese and Korean glyphs without downloading fonts at runtime. The Simplified Chinese face does not promise every regional glyph variant, script or bidirectional layout.
