# Product Hunt draft — do not publish before eligibility

## Eligibility gate

Product Hunt is last, not first. Publish only after:

- signed/notarized one-click installers exist for the advertised platforms;
- at least 8 of 10 fresh-machine non-developers complete the five-minute sample
  without help and zero P0 blocker remains;
- the release page, checksums, support path, limitations, and uninstaller work;
- the maintainer can provide same-day support without collecting private PDFs.

## Listing

**Name:** PDF Editor Offline

**Tagline:** Redact sensitive PDFs locally—and verify the data is gone.

**Description:** A local-first PDF workspace for redaction, privacy cleanup,
forms, page organization, OCR, and automation. Redact & Prove creates a separate
copy, reopens it, and reports exactly which extraction, render, object, metadata,
attachment, form, script, revision, and optional local-OCR checks passed. No
account, document upload, or document telemetry.

**First comment:** I built this around one frustrating failure mode: a PDF can
look redacted while its text or previous objects remain recoverable. The app does
not claim one universal green badge. It records the checks that actually ran,
fails closed when required evidence is missing, and keeps the report content-free.
The Trust Lab fixtures and schemas are public, and the original PDF is preserved.
I would love feedback on the five-minute synthetic workflow and any point where
the limitations are unclear.
